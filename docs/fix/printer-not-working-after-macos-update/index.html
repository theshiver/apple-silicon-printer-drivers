Printer stopped working after the macOS 27 Golden Gate update?

Nothing is wrong with your printer hardware. macOS 27 Golden Gate removes default Rosetta 2 translation and flags Intel-only vendor drivers as incompatible. Follow this diagnostic order to get printing again in under two minutes.

Still not printing? Restart the Mac once and re-plug the printer, then see troubleshooting. Wi-Fi printer? See below.

Step 1: Confirm whether you need a driver or just AirPrint

Check if your printer supports AirPrint on Wi-Fi or Ethernet: remove the old queue in Printers & Scanners, click Add, and look for Use: AirPrint. If AirPrint isn't offered—or your printer is USB-only—check your model on our home page search and install the free native Apple Silicon package above.

Step 2: Delete the old incompatible queue after installing

Installing the package adds a new printer entry ending in “Apple Silicon”, but does not delete your old queue. If you print to the old entry, you will still see “The printer software is not compatible with this device”. Remove the old queue in Printers & Scanners and print to the new one.

Step 3: Brand-specific quirks after a macOS update

  • Canon USB printers: Canon's old driver leaves a kernel extension that blocks macOS USB printing. The installer moves it aside automatically, but you must restart your Mac once, then unplug and re-plug the USB cable.
  • HP LaserJet 1018 / 1020 / P1005 / P1006: these host-based lasers require RAM firmware upload after power-on; our launchd hotplug helper and filter handle this automatically.
  • USB hubs and docks: if macOS doesn't detect the printer at all, plug the USB cable directly into the Mac (or through a simple USB-C adapter) instead of an unpowered hub.
  • Wi-Fi / Ethernet printers: the installer auto-configures USB printers only. For network printers, use Add Printer → Use: Select Software… and pick your model ending in “Apple Silicon”.

Questions

Will I lose scanning on an all-in-one printer?

This package provides printing drivers only. For scanning on older all-in-ones, check macOS Image Capture (ICA), VueScan (paid), or SANE (free).

Can I also print from my iPhone or iPad to a USB printer?

Yes. The installer includes the Print from iPhone & iPad app, which shares any printer set up on your Mac over AirPrint while the Mac stays awake.
